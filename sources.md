## Messaging logic
The deck is intentionally short and highly glanceable. It frames AIonOS as a business transformation layer for Mitsui's most strategic value pools:
1. Energy / infrastructure operations
2. Healthcare / wellness customer and patient engagement
3. Cross-business decisioning and governance
